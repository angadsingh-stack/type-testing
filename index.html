How to use the typing speed test

  1. Select a test duration: 30, 60, or 120 seconds.
  2. Read the passage displayed above the typing box.
  3. Start typing. The timer begins automatically.
  4. View your WPM, accuracy, and mistakes when the test ends.

What is WPM in a typing test?

WPM means words per minute. It is a common measure of typing speed. This tool estimates WPM by counting typed characters, including spaces, and dividing by five characters per standard word.

Tips to improve typing accuracy

  • Focus on accuracy before trying to type faster.
  • Practice regularly using different passages.
  • Keep your hands relaxed and maintain a comfortable posture.
  • Review your mistakes after every test.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this typing test free?

What is a good typing speed?

It depends on the task and your experience. Focus on consistent accuracy and gradual improvement rather than a single target.

Does this tool save my typing?

No. This version calculates your results in your browser and does not send your typed text to a server.
